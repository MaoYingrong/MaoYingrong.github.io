I’m Yingrong Mao, and you can call me Abbey. I’m a first-year PhD student in the Data Science program at the University of Virginia, advised by [YY Ahn](https://www.yyahn.com/) and [Alex Gates](https://www.alexgates.net/). Previously, I was a Pre-Doctoral Research Fellow at the Center for Science of Science and Innovation (CSSI) at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, and earned an MA in Computational Social Science at the University of Chicago, where I was affiliated with the Knowledge Lab.  

My research lies at the intersection of complex systems, collective intelligence, and the science of science. I study how individuals, teams, and organizations explore and adapt in uncertain and interdependent environments—how scientists and innovators search for new ideas, how teams coordinate diverse expertise, and how these micro-processes shape large-scale patterns of knowledge production. I hope to connect these insights to questions about human–AI collaboration, the future of work, and how shared meanings and norms evolve in digital societies.  

Please take a look at my [Projects]({{ '/Projects/' | relative_url }}) and [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}), and feel free to reach out at xce3va@virginia.edu if you’d like to discuss shared interests or potential collaborations — I’m always happy to connect.
